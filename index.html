The details, made simple

Is Young Inventors a physical product?

No. Young Inventors is a collection of digital educational materials. Craft and electronic components are not shipped.

What is included in the Essential Kit?

Over 200 projects and four bonus guides, designed for learning through building and experimenting.

What is included in the Complete Kit?

The Essential contents plus additional projects, videos, two exclusive guides and a private members area.

Do children need robotics experience?

No prior experience is required for the beginner activities. Adult supervision and support depend on the child's age and the project.

Do I need to buy materials separately?

Yes, some projects use everyday recycled items while others require basic craft or electronic components.
